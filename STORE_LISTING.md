## Full description (max 4000 characters)

```
Docfold turns paper into a clean PDF in under a minute: forms, ID cards, receipts, certificates, notes and contracts. Scan, arrange the pages, and save or share one PDF. No account, no watermark, no limits, and nothing leaves your phone unless you send it.

SCAN
• Automatic edge detection and capture — just point your camera at the page
• Fixes tilt and perspective, and removes shadows and stains
• Scan many pages in one go, or add pages later
• Works on older and low-memory phones too, with a basic camera mode and manual corners

ARRANGE
• Every page sits in a sheet like postage stamps — hold and drag to reorder
• Crop, rotate, duplicate, delete, or move pages into a new document
• Filters: Magic (clean white paper), Grayscale, Black & White
• Sign with your finger and place the signature anywhere on the page

SAVE AS PDF
• Fits upload limits: choose "Under 1 MB", "Under 500 KB" or "Under 200 KB" and Docfold sizes the PDF for you — ideal for job, exam, bank and government portals
• Page size: fit page, A4, Letter or Legal, with optional margins
• ID card layout: front and back on one sheet at real card size, like a photocopy
• Password-protect PDFs with AES-256 encryption
• Searchable PDFs: the text in your scans becomes searchable and copyable
• Save to Downloads, or share to WhatsApp, Gmail, Drive and any app
• Save pages as JPG images

MORE
• Extract text (OCR) from any page and copy or share it
• Import photos and PDFs — also the easy way to merge several PDFs
• Merge documents, search by name, and see the size of every file
• "Make a PDF with Docfold" from your gallery's share button
• Light and dark themes

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your scans are stored only on your phone. Docfold has no account and no cloud, and it does not ask for camera, storage, contacts or location permission. Scanning and text recognition run on your device.

Docfold is supported by ads. Ads never interrupt scanning or editing.

Questions or ideas? Write to us at the email below.
```
